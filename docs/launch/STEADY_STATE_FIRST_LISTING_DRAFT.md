## Short storefront summary
A practical, low-pressure journal for checking in with your current state, noticing what your energy allows and choosing one manageable next step. Designed for thoughtful self-reflection rather than perfection or rigid routines.

## Main product description (English, editorial draft)
Some days start with too much happening at once; others feel impossible to start. The Steady State is a guided journal and toolkit that makes room for both. Check in with how you feel, observe your energy without judgment, and work through one small response at a time.

Inside the workbook, the reader can use the four-state self-scan, baseline-energy check-in and deconstructed-step prompts to notice patterns and turn a difficult moment into a more approachable next action. The tone is practical, welcoming and non-punitive. The goal is reflection and everyday planning, not keeping a perfect streak.
