# Editorial QA checklist

- cover feels branded and intentional
- section titles are visually distinct from ordinary headings
- reflection prompts are visually grouped with response areas
- rating scales and checkboxes are easy to scan
- worksheet tables have clear header treatment and usable writing space
- no Gentle Page protocol syntax is reader-facing
- static and fillable versions feel like the same product
- color remains restrained and accessible
- no page looks sparse because of an unresolved compiler break
